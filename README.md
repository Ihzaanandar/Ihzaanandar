## 💡 About Me

I am an Informatics undergraduate student at **Ahmad Dahlan University** with a strong interest in **Data Engineering and Data Analytics**, supported by foundational knowledge in Machine Learning.

I have hands-on experience in **data cleaning, SQL querying, relational database design, and interactive dashboard development** using Tableau and Power BI. Through academic projects and professional experience as a **Database Lab Assistant** and **Student Employee**, I have developed strong analytical thinking and the ability to transform raw data into structured, meaningful insights.

I am passionate about building reliable, scalable, and data-driven solutions that support effective decision-making.
